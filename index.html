Research

I am a PhD candidate at the Chair of Communication Networks, University of Tübingen, supervised by Prof. Dr. habil. Michael Menth. My thesis, “Toward Deterministic Wi-Fi”, asks how Wi-Fi can serve industrial and mission-critical applications that need predictable latency.

The focus is on IEEE 802.11ax/be (Wi-Fi 6/7): Target Wake Time (TWT) and Restricted TWT (R-TWT), and how they interact with EDCA channel access. I combine analytical airtime models, large-scale discrete-event simulation and measurements to find where the standard delivers its guarantees and where it does not.

Earlier work covered time-slotted MAC scheduling for IEEE 802.15.4e TSCH in low-power industrial networks, including multipath-aware scheduling for reliability and fault tolerance.

Collaborations: Universitat Pompeu Fabra · Nokia Bell Labs · University of Naples Federico II · CNRS / University of Strasbourg
